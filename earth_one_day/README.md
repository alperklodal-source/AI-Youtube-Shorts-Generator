# If Earth's History Was One Day

A 24 h clock races through 4.54 billion years, then crawls: piecewise-linear keyframes (video s → clock s), tenths
of a second near midnight. Events (clock = (4.54 Ga − age)/4.54 Ga × 86 400 s): first life 3.8 Ga ≈ 03:55, oxygen
2.4 Ga ≈ 11:19, animals 538 Ma ≈ 21:09, dinosaurs 230 Ma ≈ 22:44, asteroid 66 Ma ≈ 23:39, Homo sapiens 300 ka ≈
23:59:54, written history ≈ last 0.1 s. Music drop aligned to "humans appear". No real dinosaur footage exists:
use mood footage (ancient forest) — never toy/CGI dinos. Render: `render_clock.py PROJECT`.
