TITLE:
What if the Moon was as close as the ISS? 🌕 #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
The Moon is 384,400 km away. The International Space Station orbits just ~400 km above us.
If the Moon were that close, it would cover about 108° of the sky — more than half of it — and race around Earth every ~2 hours.
But it would be far inside Earth's Roche limit, so tidal forces would tear it apart… and the pieces would form rings around our planet.
Would you want to see that sky? 👇
