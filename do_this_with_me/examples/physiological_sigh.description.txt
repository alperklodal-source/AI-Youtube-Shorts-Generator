TITLE:
Feeling anxious? Breathe with me (20 seconds) #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
The "physiological sigh": breathe in through your nose, take one more short sip of air, then breathe out slowly. Repeat.
In a 2023 Stanford study (Cell Reports Medicine), 5 minutes a day of this "cyclic sighing" improved mood more than mindfulness meditation.
Save this for when you need it 🤍

Not medical advice. If anxiety is overwhelming, please reach out to a professional.
